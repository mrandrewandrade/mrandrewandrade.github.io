## Résumé of Contributions

Systems practitioner focused on execution: understanding messy technical problems, reducing them to fundamentals, making useful systems work, and teaching them clearly.

I spent much of my career learning to teach machines from data. Now I teach humans who are learning to teach machines. My bias is toward basics before abstraction: arithmetic before calculus, statistics before machine learning, and the simplest useful model first.
