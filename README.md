## Patents & Inventions

### U.S. Patent 12,542,860
**Systems and Methods for Converting a Raster Image into a Plurality of Vectors**  
Sole inventor: Andrew Andrade  
Granted 2026.  
[Provisional](https://lnkd.in/gMsTSzYJ) // [Granted patent](https://lnkd.in/gxHBzDjB)

The patent is about recovering information encoded through colour in raster images, such as maps, scientific graphics, and heatmaps, and converting that information back into usable numerical or vector data.

One application I find especially meaningful is accessibility. When important information is communicated only through colour, people with colour perception deficiencies can misinterpret it. Converting that information into numerical data makes it possible to represent the same information in other ways that are easier to interpret.

The path to the patent also connects different stages of my education. My first patent drew heavily on practical manufacturing knowledge I learned working in machine shops in high school. This second patent drew on what I learned later in university: numerical methods, modelling, optimization, and the kind of earlier machine-learning and AI methods that predated the current LLM era.

The patent review also referenced undergraduate work I wrote with [Claudia Sikorski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/claudia-sikorski/), supervised by [Jeff Alstott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeffalstott/). Seeing student work reappear years later in the background of a separate invention was a particularly meaningful full-circle moment.
