# Curriculum Vitae

## Profile

Systems practitioner focused on execution across human and machine systems. My work has moved from machining and hardware, through applied research, machine learning and enterprise data systems, into technical education.

I spent much of my career learning to teach machines from data. I now teach humans who are learning to teach machines. My bias is toward fundamentals before abstraction: arithmetic before calculus, statistics before machine learning, and the simplest useful model first.

## Patents & Inventions

### U.S. Patent 12,542,860
**Systems and Methods for Converting a Raster Image into a Plurality of Vectors**  
Sole inventor: Andrew Andrade  
Granted 2026  
[Provisional](https://lnkd.in/gMsTSzYJ) // [Granted patent](https://lnkd.in/gxHBzDjB)

Recovers information encoded through colour in raster images such as maps, scientific graphics, and heatmaps and converts it back into usable numerical/vector data. One application is accessibility: information communicated only through colour can be transformed into other representations.

The invention drew on numerical methods, modelling, optimization, and earlier machine-learning methods learned at university. The patent review also referenced undergraduate work with [Claudia Sikorski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/claudia-sikorski/), supervised by [Jeff Alstott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeffalstott/).
